Expansionist Thinking

Logic, Anthroposophy and AI Research

This site presents a body of work developed through live dialogue between Martin van Walsum and AI systems — primarily Claude (Anthropic), with contributions from ChatGPT and Gemini.

The central purpose is straightforward: to demonstrate, through honest logical inquiry, that several foundational predicates of the dominant materialist framework cannot withstand examination on their own terms — using science's own evidence, science's own terminology, and logic alone as the court of appeal.

The method is called expansionist thinking — in deliberate contrast to reductionism. It moves outward from earned ground rather than inward toward assumed answers. Questions are owned. Answers are refused. Each step must be earned before the next is taken.

AI serves a precise and limited role here: not as authority or originator, but as validity checker — an instrument capable of following logic honestly without ego, institutional loyalty, or framework to defend. That combination — owned questions met with honest logical checking — makes possible a form of inquiry that was not practically available before large language models existed.

The dialogues are presented largely as they occurred — raw, including corrections and moments of redirection. That rawness is intentional. The discipline of the method is visible in the process, not just the conclusions.

The Method

Expansionist thinking moves outward from what is logically undeniable, step by earned step, without assuming the destination. AI functions here as a neutral logical witness — following chains of reasoning without institutional commitment to any framework, including the materialist one the inquiry is examining.
